[size=4][b]Detailed Weapon & Shadow Weapon Descriptions (PBZDetailedWeaponDesc)[/b][/size]

Replaces vague weapon descriptions such as "slightly increases damage" or "deals more Sha-Chi damage" with [b]exact, engine-verified numbers[/b] extracted directly from the game's BulletData, GameplayAbilities, and GameplayEffects — while adding complete combo & variant move multiplier tables to the bottom of every weapon's description panel.

[b]100% Native UI Integration[/b]: Uses the game's own RichText styles, fonts, emerald-teal numeric styling, yellow/purple Snap-Soul highlights, locked/unlocked opacity, and native UMG scroll panels. No .pak files are modified.

[line]

[size=3][b]Main Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Exact Upgrade & Passive Values (100+ Entries)[/b]: Appends exact percentages, burst multipliers, durations, and stack bonuses in parentheses at the end of upgrade milestones (Lv5 / Lv10 / Lv15 / Lv20 / Lv25 / Lv30) and Snap-Soul passives.
[*][b]Locked Tier Preview[/b]: Even before reaching Lv10 / Lv20 / Lv30, locked upgrade entries clearly display expected next-tier and max-tier values, and seamlessly use the game's native faded preview arrows once unlocked.
[*][b]Full Combo & Variant Move Tables (48 Weapons & Shadow Weapons)[/b]: Adds clean native entries at the bottom of the basic view showing exact hit counts, [b]Damage %[/b], [b]Sha-Chi %[/b], and [b]Status Buildup %[/b] for all basic combos, combo variants (XY / XXY / XXYY / XXYYY, etc.), dash/draw/dodge moves, enhanced forms, blade waves, and Shadow Weapon skills.
[*][b]Automatic Language Detection (i18n)[/b]: Automatically follows your game's active UI language ([b]English[/b], [b]Simplified Chinese[/b], and [b]Traditional Chinese[/b]).
[*][b]Live Mod Menu Toggles[/b]: Integrates cleanly with [b]PBZ Mods Menu[/b] — toggle features on or off live without restarting the game.
[/list]
